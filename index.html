02 / Behind the camera

Present for the moment.

I’m Harrison, a director of photography and documentary filmmaker based in Las Vegas.

My work lives close to people: the quiet before a fight, the conversation between takes, the moments you can’t script.

I’ve spent the past five years filming with the UFC, following athletes around the world for Embedded and documentary productions. Before that, I worked with Kentucky Athletics.

I also co-founded Overhand Films with Jake Leger, bringing our documentary experience to stories beyond sport.
